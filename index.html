What is Ollama?
Ollama lets you run open-weight AI models like LLaVA entirely on your own laptop — no cloud, no API key, no cost. WingLog uses it to identify birds from photos.

Install Steps

1️⃣ Download from ollama.com
2️⃣ Pull a vision model:
ollama pull llava
3️⃣ Start with CORS enabled:
OLLAMA_ORIGINS=* ollama serve
4️⃣ Return here and tap 🔍 Identify with AI!

Recommended Vision Models

llava (7B) — fast llava:13b — accurate llama3.2-vision — latest moondream — tiny

Why Open-Source AI?

Your bird photos and journal entries never leave your device. No subscription, no usage limits, no data harvesting. WingLog works on the trail with no internet — just your laptop and Ollama.
